Design & Delivery

Appropriate

Def: Suitable or fitting for a particular purpose.

Ensure the assessment method is genuinely fit for purpose. AI should enhance measurement, not be a default.

Accurate

Def: Exactly conforming to truth, rule or standard.

Assessment outcomes must conform to truth. AI grading engines must be regularly validated to ensure no bias or error.

Reliable &
Consistent

Def: Trustworthy; constantly adhering to principles.

Assessments must yield stable, fair results over time. Evaluate every learner against the same steady principles.

Effective

Def: Producing the intended or expected result.

The assessment must produce practical results—actually proving that the learner can perform the job.

Governance & Trust

Robust

Def: Able to withstand critical analysis & variation.

Assessments must remain functional and fair even when learners use varied AI tools or attempt to bypass guardrails.

Defensible &
Justifiable

Def: Capable of being shown to be right.

Every decision—from using AI in grading to banning certain tools—must be defendable to stakeholders and regulators.

Safeguard

Def: Something serving as a protection or defence.

Put clear measures in place to protect data privacy, prevent cheating, and ensure a safe assessment environment.

Oversight

Def: To notice or take into account; avoid omission.

Establish clear processes to monitor AI outputs and catch inadvertent errors before they impact a learner.

Accountability

Should (Obligation)

Def: Indicating obligation.

Define clear ethical obligations for L&D teams and learners regarding the transparency and honesty of AI usage.

Intervene

Def: To come between in action; intercede.

Establish "human-in-the-loop" protocols. Be ready to mediate or override systems if an AI grade is disputed.

Accountable

Def: Responsible, capable of being explained.

Decisions must be transparent. If an AI gives a grade, L&D must be able to explain exactly why and how.

Ensure &
Active

Def: To make sure. Constantly engaged, causing change.

Take proactive steps for integrity. Remain engaged, updating assessment strategies to stay relevant as AI changes.
